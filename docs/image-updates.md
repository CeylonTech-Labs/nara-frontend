# Image update prompts and provenance

Tool mode: built-in `image_gen` (no CLI/API fallback). Ingredient art is illustrative. The supplied label, not generated imagery, is the source of ingredient membership.

Final workspace assets: `public/images/products/botanical-v2-cutout.webp`, `public/images/products/rosemary-cutout.webp`, and the eleven `*-illustration.webp` files under `public/images/ingredients/`. The retained atlas is `scripts/asset-sources/ingredient-atlas.png`; transparency masks are in `scripts/asset-masks/`. Run `node scripts/prepare-clean-assets.mjs` to recreate the final derivatives.

## Bottle masks

Botanical (updated packaging, November 2026): no image generation. `scripts/make-bottle-mask.mjs` flood-fills the plain cream background (RGB 245, 233, 211) from the crop’s edges with a tolerance of 14 and keeps everything else opaque, with a one-pixel soft edge. `scripts/prepare-assets.mjs` runs it on the front-view crop of `nara-updated-img/IMG-20261102-WA0029.jpg.jpeg`.

Earlier Botanical packaging (superseded): “Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: supplied NARA Botanical Hair Oil bottle image. Remove ONLY the tan rectangular background and surface, leaving the original single amber dropper bottle isolated on genuine transparent alpha. Preserve the bottle exactly: original silhouette, glass, cap, highlights, all label artwork and all label typography, wording, colours and proportions. Do not redraw, regenerate, retype or beautify the label. No new text, frame, backdrop, platform or props. Entire bottle visible, tight portrait crop with small transparent margins. Output one transparent PNG suitable for a premium storefront.”

Rosemary (current): “Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied NARA Rosemary Oil bottle. Remove ONLY the rectangular cream background and surface, isolate the exact original amber dropper bottle on genuine transparent alpha. Preserve bottle silhouette, glass, cap, highlights and especially the original label artwork and all Sinhala and English typography exactly without redrawing or regenerating them. No new label, text, background, frame, props or platform. Entire bottle visible, tight portrait crop with small transparent margins. Output a single transparent PNG.”

Only silhouettes from these generated outputs were used. Their generated bottle RGB artwork was rejected. The masks were aligned and refined against the source edges, then applied to the original supplied RGB image crops, which are encoded losslessly. Automated tests check that every fully opaque output pixel matches the original source pixel exactly.

## Ingredient atlas prompt

“Create a premium naturalistic botanical illustration atlas for a Sri Lankan heritage beauty website: exactly 12 isolated ingredient still lifes arranged in a strict evenly spaced 4-column by 3-row grid, on genuine transparent background. Canvas 1536x1024 landscape. Each item centered within its own equal cell, generous clear margins inside every cell; no item crosses cell boundaries. No borders, no circles, no panels, no text, no letters, no numbers, no containers with labels. Consistent detailed hand-painted botanical realism with soft warm natural light, muted greens, cream, amber and earthy tones. Grid positions must be exact. ROW 1 left to right: 1 brown mature coconut half with white flesh and a small clear golden oil droplet for Virgin Coconut Oil; 2 ivory sesame seeds and sesame seed pod for Sesame Oil; 3 brown almonds with a pale almond kernel for Almond Oil; 4 castor leaves and smooth mottled castor seeds for Castor Oil. ROW 2 left to right: 1 vivid orange king coconut with white cut flesh for King Coconut Oil; 2 red hibiscus blossom with long protruding stamen and green leaf; 3 blue-violet water lily bloom with narrow pointed petals and yellow center, not a pink lotus; 4 pile of golden brown angular fenugreek seeds. ROW 3 left to right: 1 rosemary sprigs with narrow needle-like leaves; 2 a small pile of matte black nigella seeds for Black Seed; 3 gotukola sprigs with rounded scalloped kidney-shaped leaves; 4 curry leaf sprig with multiple glossy pointed leaflets. Every cell depicts only its specified ingredient, no combined neighboring items. These are ingredient illustrations, no claims, no bottles or product packaging.”

Final transparency correction: “Background-extraction correction for this botanical atlas: remove EVERY colored background, colored haze, blurry gradient, colored halo and ambient glow behind ALL twelve ingredients. The empty space between and around each ingredient must be completely clear with alpha exactly zero, not a semi-transparent colored gradient. Preserve all twelve ingredient subjects, same positions, same strict 4-column 3-row grid, same 1536x1024 canvas, same botanical details. No labels, frames, text, backgrounds, scenery or shadows. Only the crisp isolated ingredient objects on fully transparent empty pixels. This is for cropping each grid cell into a clean frameless website icon.”


The atlas still contains the curry leaf cell from the earlier label; it is no longer cropped or served, because Curry Leaves is not on the updated Botanical label.
